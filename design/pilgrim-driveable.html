Drive the Pilgrim

One generated drawing, cut into two parts. The body rotates to your heading; the lantern hangs off the arm on its own constraint and lags every turn. Still no extra frames.

Drag anywhere
heading 0 speed 0 swing 0 flex 0 felled 0

Collect the violet motes. They charge a Fate Card. When it rises the world drops to 0.06 speed and the card takes your thumb: drag it left or right and release past a third to commit. The first card sets the relic's form, every card after grows it. At level 3 hold the card still to arm its signature.

Pick a relic. Only an unarmed Pilgrim throws a straight line. The Thurible sweeps an arc across everything in front, the Passing Bell tolls a ring outward and needs no target at all, the Wild Chain throws a verlet rope whose head does the damage, and the Censer-Rot lobs a blot that leaves a pool eating whatever stands in it.

Press Signature. Every foe is marked at once. Then watch an ordinary shot with Recoil on and off: the body kicks back, the hood glances at the target, the lantern lurches and a ripple crosses the hem. There are no attack frames — the rig does all of it.

Turn Cloak off, then on. Off, the drawing is rigid. On, it is sliced into twelve wedges around the shoulders, each on its own damped spring, lagging the turn and streaming behind the direction of travel. The hood and shoulders stay solid. Same one drawing.
